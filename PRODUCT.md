# SYNAPSE Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

SYNAPSE serves university administrators, audit readers, clinic staff, counsellors, facilities operators, report viewers, employees, and students. The administration audit surface is primarily for administrators and audit readers who investigate activity, correlate events with requests and actors, verify chain integrity, and export evidence for operational or compliance review.

## Product Purpose

SYNAPSE unifies campus clinic, counselling, referral, facilities, inventory, reporting, and governance workflows. It should make sensitive operational work traceable and dependable while preserving clear module boundaries and least-privilege access. Success means users can complete high-consequence tasks accurately, understand system state without guesswork, and produce trustworthy evidence when activity is reviewed.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, institutional.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy consumer analytics, decorative healthcare-dashboard tropes, opaque terminal-style log viewers, and novelty interactions that make evidence harder to scan. Do not use visual drama, ornamental motion, or color as a substitute for explicit labels and hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before decoration: make identity, time, scope, and integrity legible at a glance.
2. Progressive disclosure: keep the event list scannable and reveal forensic detail on demand.
3. Safe defaults: redact sensitive payloads, preserve read-only behavior, and make consequential actions explicit.
4. Familiar administration patterns: use predictable filters, tables, status language, keyboard behavior, and export controls.
5. URL-addressable investigations: filters and entity context should survive refreshes and be shareable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All controls and event inspection flows must support keyboard operation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, color-independent status communication, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.
